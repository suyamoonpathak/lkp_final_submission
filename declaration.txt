Attributions (external sources used):
  - Linux 6.1.4 kernel source, downloaded from
    https://cdn.kernel.org/pub/linux/kernel/v6.x/linux-6.1.4.tar.xz .
    Suyamoon's patches (Fast_Commit_Optimization/fc-inline-xattr.patch and
    Fast_Commit_Optimization/fc-fallocate-range.patch) modify fs/ext4/xattr.c,
    fs/ext4/fast_commit.c, and fs/ext4/extents.c under the kernel's
    existing GPL v2 license. Sahil's out-of-tree kernel module
    (Lockless_CAS_Optimization/artifact_files/jbd2_trace.c) hooks into JBD2 via the kernel's
    public kretprobes API, also under GPL v2. Shrey's two patches
    (Fragmeted_Jounral_Map_Optimization/patches/First.patch, Second.patch) modify
    fs/jbd2/journal.c, fs/jbd2/transaction.c, and
    include/linux/jbd2.h to add allocator and free-txn callback
    hooks; his out-of-tree ext4_tracker module
    (Fragmeted_Jounral_Map_Optimization/module/ext4_tracker.tar.gz) is also under GPL v2.
  - The ATC 2024 FastCommit paper by Shirwadkar, Kadekodi, and Ts'o
    was referenced for design context and its identification of the
    xattr coverage gap that Candidate 3 closes.
  - The CJFS (FAST 2023), iJournaling (ATC 2017), and Optimistic
    Crash Consistency (SOSP 2013) papers were referenced as related
    work in the report.
  - xfstests from
    https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/fs/xfs/xfstests-dev.git
    was used unmodified for correctness evaluation.
  - The fio benchmarking tool (https://fio.readthedocs.io) was used
    unmodified across multiple members' benchmark scripts.
  - The bpftrace tracing tool (https://bpftrace.org) was used
    unmodified to instrument JBD2 tracepoints during the
    workload characterization.
  - sysbench, fs_mark, and dbench were used unmodified by Sahil for
    the multi-thread scaling matrix.
  - AI assistance (Claude, ChatGPT, etc) was used by individual members for
    brainstorming patch ideas, drafting benchmark scripts, and
    proofreading the report. All final patch code, benchmark
    scripts, and prose were reviewed, tested, and approved by the
    project members before submission.
