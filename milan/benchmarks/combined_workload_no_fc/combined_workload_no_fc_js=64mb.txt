==================================================================================
  INTERPRETATION GUIDE
==================================================================================

  T1  Throughput: IOPS and bandwidth per mode, per job.
  T2  Latency: per-I/O round-trip including any fsync() cost.
  T3  Overhead %: performance penalty of journaling vs nojournal baseline.
  T4  IOPS variability: high CV% means journal checkpoint stall spikes.
  T5  Latency split: fraction of time in fsync() vs write().
  T6  CPU: kernel (JBD2 commit, writeback) vs user time per job.
  T7  WAF: combined physical bytes / nojournal -- total journaling tax.
  T8  JBD2 tracing: commit counts, stage timing, % bench time in journal.
  X1  Side-by-side throughput showing concurrent contention effects.
